Creator-controlled workflows for short-form video.

FuturLab helps creators prepare, review and publish short-form videos about technology, science, innovation and the future. Publishing actions remain under the creator's control and are limited to owned, licensed, campaign-provided or otherwise authorized content.

What FuturLab provides

Video preparation

Short-form content is organized for platform-ready publishing workflows, including review of the video and its publishing metadata.

Creator review

Creators can preview the selected video, edit captions and review available privacy and interaction settings before posting.

Authorized publishing

Connected creators explicitly approve each publishing action through supported platform integrations.

How the publishing workflow works

1. Connect an account. The creator authorizes FuturLab through the platform's official authentication flow.
2. Choose and review a video. The creator selects authorized content and previews it before upload.
3. Review publishing settings. The creator reviews caption, privacy and available interaction options.
4. Confirm the action. Nothing is published until the creator explicitly confirms the upload or direct post.
5. Track processing. FuturLab reports the platform processing status so the creator can confirm completion.

Authorized content only

FuturLab is designed for content that the creator owns or has permission to use. We do not provide fake engagement, artificial views or unauthorized redistribution. Creators are responsible for confirming that they have the necessary rights before submitting content for publishing.
