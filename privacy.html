FuturLab Privacy Policy

FuturLab processes only the information needed to provide authorized content management and publishing workflows.

Authentication and authorization data received from connected platforms is used only to provide the functionality explicitly authorized by the account owner.

FuturLab does not sell personal data to advertisers. Access credentials, tokens and platform authorization data are not intentionally exposed publicly.

Users may request information about their stored data or request deletion by contacting FuturLab.
