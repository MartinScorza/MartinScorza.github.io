Share to TikTok

Connect your TikTok account, review the video and settings, then either post directly or send the video to TikTok as a draft.

Connected TikTok account

1. Choose and preview video

For this sandbox demo, use a short video you own or are authorized to publish.

2. Direct Post settings


TikTok requires the creator to review all settings and explicitly consent before Direct Post.

3. Confirm and share



Direct Post uses video.publish. Draft Upload uses video.upload and requires the creator to finish editing/posting from the TikTok inbox notification.
